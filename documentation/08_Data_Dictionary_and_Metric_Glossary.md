# Enterprise Insurance Intelligence Platform

# Data Dictionary & Metric Glossary

Version: 1.0


# 1. Purpose


A strong analytics platform requires standardized business definitions.

Without a governed metric framework, different teams may interpret the same business concept differently, resulting in:

- inconsistent reporting
- conflicting decisions
- reduced trust in analytics


This document establishes a common business language for insurance analytics.


The objective is:

**One Metric → One Definition → One Source of Truth**


---

# 2. Data Dictionary Framework


Each analytical asset is documented using:


| Attribute | Description |
|-|-|
| Data Asset | Name of table/view/entity |
| Business Purpose | Why the asset exists |
| Data Domain | Business area |
| Owner | Responsible function |
| Sensitivity | Data classification |
| Usage | Analytical consumption |

# 4. Enterprise Metric Glossary


# Revenue Metrics


---

# Premium Contribution


## Definition

Total premium value generated by policies within a specific business segment.


Formula:


```

Premium Contribution

=

Sum of Premium Amount

```id="4q5mgv"


Business Purpose:

Measures revenue impact.


Used By:

- Finance
- Executive Leadership


Decision Supported:

Which regions and products generate business value?


---

# Revenue Quality


## Definition

Measurement of sustainable revenue generation beyond policy volume.


Considers:

- premium value
- customer quality
- policy sustainability indicators


Business Purpose:

Distinguishes healthy growth from volume-driven growth.


Used By:

Executive Leadership


Decision Supported:

Are we growing profitably?


---

# Policy Growth


## Definition

Increase in active insurance policies over a defined period.


Formula:


```

Current Active Policies

*

Previous Active Policies

```id="axw1kr"


Business Purpose:

Measures business expansion.

# 6. Sales Metrics


# Agent Productivity


## Definition

Business contribution generated by an insurance agent.


Possible measures:

- policies generated
- premium contribution
- conversion outcomes


Business Purpose:

Evaluate sales effectiveness.


---

# Agent Dependency Ratio


## Definition

Measure of revenue concentration among top-performing agents.


Business Purpose:

Identify business continuity risks.


Example:

If a small number of agents generate most revenue:

Risk:

High dependency.


---

# Conversion Rate


## Definition

Percentage of qualified leads converted into policies.


Formula:


```

Converted Leads

---

Qualified Leads

```id="c7w9lz"


Business Purpose:

Measure acquisition effectiveness.


Used By:

Marketing and Sales Teams

# 10. Metric Governance Rules


## Rule 1

Every KPI must have:

- business definition
- calculation logic
- owner


---

## Rule 2

Metrics should be created once and reused.


---

## Rule 3

Business users should consume governed metrics instead of creating personal calculations.


---

## Rule 4

Metric changes require business validation.

# 12. Business Value


A governed metric framework provides:


## Consistent Decision Making

Different teams use the same definitions.


## Faster Analytics Delivery

Analysts spend less time debating calculations.


## Improved Trust

Leadership receives reliable insights.


## Scalable Analytics

New dashboards can reuse existing business definitions.


---

# Metric Governance Philosophy


A mature analytics organization does not ask:

"Can we calculate this metric?"


It asks:

"Does the organization agree on what this metric means?"

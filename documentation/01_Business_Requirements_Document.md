# 1. Executive Summary

## Business Vision

Insurance organizations operate in a highly data-intensive environment where customer information, policy transactions, agent performance, underwriting decisions, and financial outcomes are deeply interconnected.

However, organizations often struggle to convert operational data into reliable business intelligence due to:

* fragmented data sources
* inconsistent business definitions
* limited data governance
* poor visibility into customer behaviour
* disconnected reporting systems

The Enterprise Insurance Intelligence Platform was designed to establish a trusted analytics foundation that enables:

**Reliable Data → Business Intelligence → Strategic Decision Making**

The platform transforms raw insurance data into governed analytical assets supporting:

* revenue intelligence
* customer analytics
* sales optimization
* underwriting intelligence
* executive decision-making

---

# 2. Business Objective

The primary objective is to design an enterprise analytics ecosystem that enables insurance stakeholders to make faster and more accurate decisions using trusted data.

The platform aims to answer critical business questions:

| Business Area           | Key Question                                                           |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Revenue Growth          | Are we achieving sustainable growth or only increasing policy volumes? |
| Customer Experience     | Where are customer trust gaps impacting conversions?                   |
| Sales Performance       | Which agents create long-term business value?                          |
| Marketing Effectiveness | Where does the customer funnel break?                                  |
| Risk Management         | Are underwriting decisions aligned with future liability?              |
| Data Governance         | Can business teams trust the data they consume?                        |

# 4. Current State Challenges

## Problem Statement

Although insurance organizations collect extensive operational data, the absence of an integrated analytical platform creates decision-making challenges.

---

## Challenge 1: Data Fragmentation

Customer, policy, agent, underwriting, and lead data exist across different operational areas.

Impact:

* inconsistent reporting
* duplicate analysis efforts
* limited customer visibility

---

## Challenge 2: Lack of Data Trust

Business users cannot confidently rely on analytical outputs due to:

* missing values
* inconsistent attributes
* duplicate records
* non-standardized fields

Impact:

Incorrect business decisions and reduced confidence in analytics.

---

## Challenge 3: Volume-Based Decision Making

Traditional insurance reporting focuses on:

* policies sold
* leads generated
* conversion percentage

However, organizations require:

* revenue quality
* customer lifetime value
* risk-adjusted growth

---

## Challenge 4: Limited Customer Intelligence

Insurance requires understanding customer journeys.

Current gaps:

* fragmented customer history
* unclear trust barriers
* limited behavioural insights

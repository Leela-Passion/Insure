# 1. BI Vision

## Transforming Data into Actionable Business Intelligence


The purpose of the Business Intelligence layer is not simply to visualize data.

It is designed to convert trusted analytical assets into decision-making capabilities for business stakeholders.


The BI layer enables insurance leaders to understand:

- where growth is occurring
- whether growth is sustainable
- how customers behave
- how sales teams perform
- where operational risks exist

# 3. Business Semantic Layer


## Purpose

Create a common business language across departments.


A semantic layer ensures that metrics are:

- consistently defined
- reusable
- trusted
- aligned with business objectives


---

# Example Business Metrics


## Premium Growth


Business Definition:

Change in premium contribution over time.


Used By:

- Finance
- Executive Leadership


Business Question:

Are we achieving sustainable revenue growth?


---

## Conversion Rate


Business Definition:

Percentage of qualified leads converted into policies.


Used By:

- Marketing
- Sales Leadership


Business Question:

Where are customer acquisition journeys failing?


---

## Agent Productivity


Business Definition:

Business contribution generated by sales representatives.


Used By:

- Sales Leadership


Business Question:

Which behaviours create sustainable sales performance?

# 4. Enterprise 360 Analytics Framework


The platform creates three major analytical perspectives:


# Customer 360


## Objective

Create a unified view of customer relationships.


Combines:

- customer information
- policy relationships
- protection details
- engagement behaviour


---

## Business Questions


- Who are our highest-value customers?
- Which customers require targeted engagement?
- How can retention improve?


---

## Business Value


Enables:

- customer segmentation
- personalized engagement
- relationship management


---

# Policy 360


## Objective

Provide complete visibility into policy performance.


Combines:

- policy information
- premium details
- coverage information
- customer relationship


---

## Business Questions


- Which policies drive revenue?
- Which products have stronger adoption?
- Where are profitability risks emerging?


---

## Business Value


Enables:

- revenue analysis
- product intelligence
- portfolio optimization


---

# Agent 360


## Objective

Understand the complete sales ecosystem.


Combines:

- agent information
- regional hierarchy
- sales outcomes
- lead conversion


---

## Business Questions


- Which agents create sustainable value?
- Are sales results concentrated?
- What behaviours should be replicated?


---

## Business Value


Enables:

- sales coaching
- incentive optimization
- performance improvement

# 5. Dashboard Strategy
